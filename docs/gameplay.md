# Prototype gameplay

The prototype randomizes items and overworld layouts, with broader gameplay verification still outstanding. The following item and restart behavior applies to patched seeds.

## Items

Swords, shields, gloves, mail, and boomerangs upgrade one level per pickup, capped at the highest tier. Bomb and arrow capacity upgrades each add 5 capacity (up to 99, from starting capacities set in the settings, 10 bombs and 30 arrows by default) along with 5 ammo. The Lake Hylia fairy fills an empty bottle with a fairy or golden bee, for free and any number of times. Visible pickups show the next upgrade. When both are owned, highlight Mushroom/Powder or Shovel/Ocarina in the item menu and press Y to switch between them.

Small and Big Keys remain at vanilla key locations, and Waterfall/Pyramid fairy exchanges stay fixed. Keys and fairy exchanges still use vanilla ROM behavior, which differs from the logical model's door-specific Small Keys and progressive equipment. Silver Arrows also grants the bow. Equipment receipt messages retain vanilla text pending replacement.

## Saving and restarting

Save-and-quit and outdoor/cave deaths lose every follower and retain the current world regardless of Agahnim progress. Light World restarts offer Link's House, Sanctuary after entering it, and Mountain Cave after reaching Light World Death Mountain. Dark World restarts offer the Pyramid, plus Link's House when the Mirror is missing.

The menu uses English labels and substitutes the saved player name in the house option. A single available destination loads directly. Dungeon deaths retain their entrance restart and vanilla follower handling, including retaining Zelda or the Old Man; Ganon's room uses outdoor recovery and loses every follower.

The Sanctuary priest remains alive and available for healing and Zelda's rescue regardless of sword level or Agahnim progress.
