## Reference: overworld-related ROM banks

### Scope

This is a survey of the `bank_*.asm` files for code or data explicitly dedicated to the playable outdoor overworld or to the full-screen Light World/Dark World map. It was generated by AI and may be incomplete and/or inaccurate; it is intended only as a starting point for investigating and understanding overworld-related behavior.

Source links target the `jpdasm/` checkout at revision `d078add` (`big update`).

### How the main pieces fit together

1. Bank 00 dispatches to the overworld modules and supplies low-level tile, NMI, graphics, palette, and music-loading services.
2. Bank 02 loads a screen and follows Map32 pointers into banks 0B/0C; it decompresses those streams and expands tiles using bank 0F's Map16 definitions.
3. Bank 0C's palette tables and bank 02's loader establish the screen appearance; bank 02 then manages the camera, scrolling, transitions, and incremental map updates.
4. Bank 09 populates the resulting screen with sprites. Banks 06 and the other sprite banks provide shared behavior and collision support.
5. Bank 07 interprets Link's contact with outdoor terrain, while bank 1B resolves entrances, secrets, destructible terrain, and persistent/animated map changes. Bank 04 records or triggers a smaller set of special changes and transitions.
6. Separately, bank 0A implements the full-screen map/flute UI using Mode 7 graphics from bank 18; selecting a flute destination hands control back to the normal bank 02 overworld-loading path.
